The Equipment Desk

The Equipment Desk is the software KH Advanced Solutions provides to construction teams. It is the working list of equipment on a job, from the request to the vendor through the call-off.

Open The Equipment Desk

What a project lead does

  • Request a type of equipment for a job number.
  • Check a unit in with photos when it arrives.
  • See what is still on site, including the vendor, purchase order, and unit number.
  • Start a call-off when the unit should leave, with pickup photos.
  • Open a service call if the unit is not working, and confirm when it is fixed.

What the equipment manager does

  • Send a new request to the vendor and record the purchase order and equipment number.
  • Correct the equipment type if the request came in under the wrong name.
  • Confirm a call-off with the vendor and close the unit.
  • Schedule a service call and follow it until the project lead confirms the unit is fixed.
  • Keep the on-site list, the log of closed orders, and the photo record for each unit.

The Equipment Desk is available to signed-in users at ed.khadvancedsolutions.com. It is a product of KH Advanced Solutions.
