How KH Advanced Solutions handles information

This page covers KH Advanced Solutions and The Equipment Desk at ed.khadvancedsolutions.com.

The Equipment Desk stores the account name a person uses to sign in, the job numbers assigned to a project lead, equipment requests, vendor and purchase-order details, equipment numbers, check-in and call-off photos, and service notes. Those records are kept so the equipment manager and the project lead can run the job.

If text or email alerts are turned on, KH Advanced Solutions uses the cell number or email address the equipment manager enters, only to send notices about open To-do items. Those addresses are not sold.

This public website does not ask visitors to create an account. Questions can be sent to koehnj@khadvancedsolutions.com or by phone at (405) 875-5958.
