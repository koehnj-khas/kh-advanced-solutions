How KH Advanced Solutions handles information

This public website does not ask visitors to create an account.

The Equipment Desk, at ed.khadvancedsolutions.com, stores sign-in names, job numbers, equipment requests, vendor and purchase-order details, equipment numbers, check-in and call-off photos, and service notes so a project lead and an equipment manager can run the job.

If text or email alerts are turned on, KH Advanced Solutions uses only the number or address the equipment manager enters, and only to send notices about open items. Those addresses are not sold.
