About the company

KH Advanced Solutions

KH Advanced Solutions builds practical software for construction operations. The work sits between the project lead in the field and the equipment manager who deals with the rental vendor.

A job site can have dozens of rented units. Each one has a type, a vendor, a purchase order, and an equipment number. Someone has to know when it arrived, what it looked like, whether it needs service, and when it should be called off. That record is easy to lose in texts, emails, and photo rolls.

KH Advanced Solutions is not a rental company and does not supply the machines. We provide the record. The Equipment Desk is our product for that job. Project leads request equipment, check it in with photos, and start a call-off or a service call. The equipment manager confirms the vendor side and keeps the on-site list current.

The company operates in the United States. Questions about KH Advanced Solutions or The Equipment Desk can be sent to koehnj@khadvancedsolutions.com or by phone at (405) 875-5958.
