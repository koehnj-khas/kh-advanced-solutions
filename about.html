About KHAS

Construction technology, one gap at a time.

KH Advanced Solutions is a United States company that builds apps, web tools, and hardware for concrete contractors.

Why we exist

The seams between the field and the office.

Construction already has the people and the machines. What it often lacks is a clean way to carry information from the office to the field and back. A request gets lost. A unit sits on site after it should have been called off. Nobody can say who asked for what.

The big platforms cover a lot, but they leave those seams open. We build focused tools that close them.

Find the gapWe learn how your project managers, supers, and field crews already work, and where that work breaks down.
Build the fixAn app, a web tool, or hardware that fits the work instead of asking the crew to change everything.
Keep improvingWe stay with the tool after launch and adjust it as your jobs change.
What we are not

We build the technology, not the job.

KH Advanced Solutions is not a general contractor and does not supply equipment or tools. We build the software and hardware around that work.
