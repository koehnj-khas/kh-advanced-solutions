About the company

KH Advanced Solutions

KH Advanced Solutions is a United States company. We bring construction technology to the industry, one problem at a time.

Construction already has the people and the machines. What it often lacks is a clean way to carry information from the office to the field and back. A request gets lost. A drawing is the wrong revision. A tool leaves the site and nobody can say when.

We work across the parts of a project where that happens: administration, document control, and technical CAD. The method is the same in each case. Find the problem. Learn how the team already works. Build a technology solution that fits that work instead of asking the crew to change everything.

KH Advanced Solutions is not a general contractor and does not supply equipment or tools. We build the software around that work. Questions can be sent to koehnj@khadvancedsolutions.com or by phone at (405) 875-5958.
