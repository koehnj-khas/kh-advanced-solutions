Construction technology · United States

Better technology for the way construction actually works.

KH Advanced Solutions looks for the problems that slow a job down, then builds a practical way to solve them. The work covers the office and the field: administration, document control, and technical CAD.

Administration

Schedules, requests, and follow-up should not live in a stack of texts. We look for the steps a team repeats and put them in one place.

Document control

Drawings, photos, and records have to stay with the job they belong to. We treat that record as part of the work, not an afterthought.

Technical CAD

The people producing the drawings and the people building from them need the same facts. Technology should close that gap, not add another file to hunt for.

The vision

Better construction technology starts with a real problem. KH Advanced Solutions studies how a crew, a project lead, or an office already works, then fits a tool to that work. The aim is simple software a job site will actually use.
