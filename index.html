Construction operations · United States

Equipment records for the job site, kept in one place.

KH Advanced Solutions works with construction teams on the rented equipment they need in the field. We build software that connects the project lead on site with the equipment manager in the office.

See The Equipment Desk

Requests

A project lead asks for a piece of equipment by job. The manager sees the request, the type, and who asked for it, then sends it to the vendor.

On site

Each unit keeps its vendor, purchase order, and equipment number. Check-in photos show the condition when it arrived.

Call-off and service

When the unit is ready to leave, pickup photos go with the call-off. If it breaks, the service call stays with that same unit.

The company

KH Advanced Solutions is a United States company. Our product, The Equipment Desk, is used by project leads and equipment managers to follow a unit from the first request through the vendor call-off.
