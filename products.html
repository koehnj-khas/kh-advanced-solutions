What we are building

Each product starts from a problem we have seen on a job. Some are in use. Some are still being built.

The Equipment Desk

In use · a product of KH Advanced Solutions

The Equipment Desk is for the rented equipment on a job. A project lead requests a unit, checks it in with photos, and starts a call-off or a service call. The equipment manager sends the request to the vendor and keeps the on-site list, the purchase order, and the unit number together.

It is a product of KH Advanced Solutions. Signed-in users open it at ed.khadvancedsolutions.com.

The Tool Vault

In development · a product of KH Advanced Solutions

The Tool Vault starts as a standard steel conex container and becomes a locked, intelligent storage unit built for a construction site.

  • Reinforced doors and locking systems
  • UHF RFID that tracks tools going in and out
  • Cameras that can identify faces and license plates
  • Starlink Mini for a connection when the site has no reliable network
  • Solar and battery power so the vault runs on its own

The container protects high-value tools and shows what is inside in real time. Concrete, steel, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, and other subcontractors can watch their gear from anywhere. That cuts theft and ends the search for a tool nobody can find.

Smart, secure tool vaults built for the way a job site actually works.

The Schedule Desk

In development · a product of KH Advanced Solutions

The Schedule Desk is scheduling software built for subcontractors, not for a head office that never walks the job. Concrete, steel, electrical, plumbing, and HVAC crews plan the work they are actually building, by the day and by the crew.

Traditional scheduling programs were written for someone else. The Schedule Desk leaves that behind. A foreman can see what is next, what is waiting on another trade, and what slipped, without a class on how to use the software.
