What we are building

Each product starts from a problem we have seen on a job. Some are in use. Some are still being built.

The Equipment Desk

In use

The Equipment Desk is for the rented equipment on a job. A project lead requests a unit, checks it in with photos, and starts a call-off or a service call. The equipment manager sends the request to the vendor and keeps the on-site list, the purchase order, and the unit number together.

It is a product of KH Advanced Solutions. Signed-in users open it at ed.khadvancedsolutions.com.

Tool management

In development

Tools walk off a site because storage is open and nobody has a current count. KH Advanced Solutions is building a tool-management product that ties smart locks and on-site storage to a simple record of who took a tool and whether it came back.

The aim is theft prevention without a complicated system: a locked crib, a clear checkout, and a list the superintendent can trust at the end of the day.
